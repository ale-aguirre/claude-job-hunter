## Delivery verification

The applier re-reads the page after submitting and records the outcome. This is
the eval signal: `CONFIRMED` means a success state was observed, `UNVERIFIED`
means the click landed but nothing proved the submission went through.

| Channel | Sent | Confirmed | Unproven | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ashby | 174 | 174 | 0 | 100% |
| Greenhouse | 52 | 52 | 0 | 100% |
| Himalayas | 18 | 18 | 0 | 100% |
| Jobicy | 4 | 4 | 0 | 100% |
| Arbeitnow | 60 | 0 | 60 | 0% |
| Direct email | 8 | — | — | n/a (no page to verify) |

**Finding:** the 78.5% aggregate confirmation rate is not a general reliability
figure. It is four channels at 100% and one channel at 0%. Every unproven
submission came from Arbeitnow. The aggregate number hid a single broken
integration — which is the entire argument for measuring per channel instead of
in total.

Direct email is reported as `n/a`, not 0%: there is no page to re-read, so a
delivery rate would be a fabricated metric. Verifying email delivery needs a
different signal (bounce handling, reply detection) that the system does not
implement.

## Orchestrator reliability

505 tasks dispatched across five worker types.

| Task type | Completed | Failed | Paused | Success |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| apply_from_db | 136 | 0 | 0 | 100% |
| ats_apply | 131 | 5 | 0 | 95.6% |
| scout | 107 | 13 | 19 | 75.4% |
| xreddit | 34 | 4 | 8 | 72.3% |
| register | 8 | 33 | 0 | 19.0% |

**Finding:** the application workers are reliable (95-100%). The failures are
concentrated in acquisition and account registration. `register` at 19% is not
a flaky integration, it is a design problem: the agent tries to create accounts
on platforms that require a real logged-in session it does not have. It should
be disabled rather than retried.

All 56 recorded task errors share one root cause: a command invoked with a
resolved path that did not exist in the worker's context.

## Self-recovery

The QA auditor ran 409 verdicts (324 pass, 85 error) and opened 3 pause signals
on its own, without human input:

- `xreddit` paused after 3 failures in 3 h — resolved 2026-04-16
- `scout` paused after 4 failures in 3 h (×2) — resolved 2026-04-22

Each pause was resolved automatically on the next clean cycle. The auditor is
what keeps a broken worker from burning the whole run: it stops that agent and
lets the rest of the pipeline continue.

## Coverage gap (open)

- Last application sent: **2026-04-28**
- Last lead scouted: **2026-07-22**
- Leads found since June and never acted on: **112**

Discovery kept running for three months after the application stage stopped.
The system has no alert for "one stage of the pipeline is dead while the others
keep working" — the single most valuable thing to add next.

## What this evaluation changes

1. Fix or drop the Arbeitnow integration — it accounts for 100% of unproven submissions.
2. Disable `register` — 19% success is a design fault, not a bug to retry.
3. Deprioritise X/Reddit scouting — 7.9% yield for the highest operational cost.
4. Add a stage-liveness check — the failure that cost the most was silent, not loud.
